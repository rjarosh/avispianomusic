Syntax

Title: Subtitle: Composer: Footer: set the header and footer. Separate systems (lines of music) with a blank line. Each has optional RH:, LH:, LYR: lines. A line with just ! adds a final double bar.

Page breaks: put PAGEBREAK on its own line to start the following music on a new printed page.

Notes: E D5 A3 (octave optional: RH defaults to 4, LH to 3). Finger numbers can be added with a hyphen: C-1 D-2 E-3. The older C/1 syntax is also supported. Barlines: |.

Repeats: !: starts a repeat at the beginning of a measure. :! ends a repeat at the end of a measure. For example: RH: C D E F :!

Rests: R:1 whole, R:2 half, R:4 quarter, R:8 eighth; dotted with a dot, e.g. R:4.. A rest that fills the bar is centered. Prefix a rest with H to hide it while keeping its rhythmic space, e.g. HR:4, HR:2.

Hide note letters: add L to the end of a note. For example, C LC LC C shows the C on the first and last notes but hides the letters on the two middle notes. This also works with durations and ties, e.g. E:2L or E:2~L.

Ties: put ~ after a note to tie it to the next note of the same pitch, even across a barline: E:2~ E:2.

Lengths: :1 whole, :2 half, :4 quarter (default), :8 eighth. Add . to dot: :2. dotted half (3 beats), :4. dotted quarter (1½). Two eighths together on a beat (C:8 D:8) are beamed automatically.

Lyrics: one syllable per note in time order, across both hands (notes that sound together share one syllable). - joins syllables, _ skips a note. To put lyrics on one hand only, use LYR RH: or LYR LH: instead.
